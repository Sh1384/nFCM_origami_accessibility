Figure 1. (A) Schematic of the DNA origami box without coating (bare). (B) The box with a PLL-PEG polymer coating and with a silica coating. (C) Antibodies bind the DNA box through a biotin-modified DNA strand hybridized to an extension on its outer face. (D) Nano-flow cytometry simultaneously measures side scatter, origami fluorescence, and antibody fluorescence of individual DNA origami particles, antibodies, and debris.
