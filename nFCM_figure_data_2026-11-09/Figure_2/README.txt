Figure 2. (A) Negative-stain TEM of bare origami: a raw field (scale bar, 100 nm) and class averages of top views (n = 129) and side views (n = 85; scale bar, 10 nm). (B) VSSC-1-H vs V525-H density plots of scatter-triggered events from buffer and from a representative bare-origami replicate (batch 2, replicate B), on the same axes and color scale; the dashed line marks the trigger threshold. (C) Side-scatter distribution of scatter-triggered events from bare origami; the red dashed line marks the trigger threshold (VSSC-1-H = 200). Inset: fraction of recorded events at or above a side scatter x, on log axes, with a guide proportional to x−3.7. (D) Rates of scatter-triggered events (VSSC-1-H ≥ 200) for buffer and bare origami without probe; bars show mean ± SD and points are individual acquisitions from two batches (n = 2 for buffer, n = 6 for origami). (E) V525-H (ATTO 425) distributions of scatter-triggered events from bare origami and buffer (batch 2).
