Caption (11/09 draft):
Figure 3. (A) Negative-stain TEM class averages of bare, PLL-PEG-coated and silica-coated origami in side and top views at identical scale (n, number of boxes; scale bar, 10 nm). (B) Intensity profiles across the side walls of the side-view averages, scaled from the cavity minimum (0) to the mean wall peak (1), with the apparent wall width (full width at half maximum), cavity width and wall spacing. (C) Simulated side-scatter intensity of PLL(200)-g[5]-PEG(5k)-coated origami relative to bare origami as a function of the fraction f of DNA phosphates bound by the polymer; dashed line, (1 + 3.49f)². (D) Simulated side-scatter intensity of silica-coated origami relative to bare origami as a function of silica thickness (conformal layer on all exposed surfaces, n = 1.45; dashed line, (1 + 0.561t)², t in nm). In (C) and (D), points are discrete-dipole (ADDA) simulations of the open-top box at 405 nm in water, and the dashed line marks no change. (E) Median side scatter (VSSC-1-H) of scatter-triggered events for bare, PLL-PEG-coated, and silica-coated origami from two independent preparations (batches 1 and 2); bars show means and points individual acquisitions. (F) Schematic: only the upper tail of the scatter distribution is recorded, so a k-fold brighter population raises the number of recorded events about k3.7-fold while the recorded median barely changes. (G) Scatter-triggered events per second for the same samples (log scale), after subtraction of buffer events (34 and 59 events/s in batches 1 and 2).(H) Expected ratio of event rates, coated to bare, for a k-fold scatter gain (R = k3.7; line), with the observed ratios (symbols at left). The gray band marks ratios within twofold of bare origami, which limits k to 1.21 or less.
