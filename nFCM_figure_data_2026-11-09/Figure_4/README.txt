Caption (11/09 draft):
Figure 4. (A) How populations appear in side scatter versus ATTO 425: particulates without dye, single origami near the trigger, coated origami shifted by their scatter gain, and aggregates rising with slope 1 (loose aggregates or coincidences) or 1/2 (compact clusters). (B) Representative VSSC-1-H vs V525-H density plot of silica-coated origami: origami events sit at low scatter, whereas silica particulates form a high-scatter, low-fluorescence population. (C) Representative density plot of PLL-PEG-coated origami (batch 2): a high-scatter, high-fluorescence branch with a log–log slope near 1 (solid guide; dashed guide, slope 1/2) indicates loose aggregates or coincident origami. (D,E) Median ATTO 425 (V525-H) signal as a function of side scatter (VSSC-1-H bin) for bare origami, silica-coated origami, silica precursors without origami, and buffer in batch 1 (D) and batch 2 (E). (F,G) The same analysis for bare origami, PLL-PEG-coated origami, PLL-PEG polymer without origami, and buffer in batch 1 (F) and batch 2 (G).
