Caption (11/09 draft):
Figure 5. (A) Schematic of two-color coincidence detection for antibody functionalization of DNA origami. (B) Antibody signal of events in the origami scatter regime versus that of low-scatter events in the same sample, for origami with and without biotin. The dashed line is a linear fit. (C) B525-H distribution of antibody-only events; the red dashed line marks the fluorescence trigger threshold (250). (D) Log2 fold change of the observed-to-chance coincidence ratio relative to the matched no-biotin control, a measure of biotin-specific binding per unit of antibody event rate, for bare and PLL-PEG-coated origami with 3, 6, and 12 bp stems. (E) Biotin-specific coincidence excess, the percentage of detected origami with a coincident antibody relative to the matched no-biotin control, for the same samples; a coincident antibody is not necessarily a bound antibody. In (D) and (E), points show the mean and error bars the SD of three technical replicates (two for PLL-PEG-coated origami with the 12 bp stem). (F) Apparent energetic penalty ΔU of the PLL-PEG coating calculated from the per-antibody fold change (Equation 6) and from the per-origami excess (Equation 5), using the stem lengths measured for both coatings in each technical replicate; bars show mean ± SD and points are the three technical replicates.
