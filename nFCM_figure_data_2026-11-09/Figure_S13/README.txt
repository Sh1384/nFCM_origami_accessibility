Figure S13. Observed-to-chance coincidence between origami and anti-biotin IgG. Ratio of observed coincident events to the number expected by chance for bare and PLL-PEG-coated origami without biotin (N) and with biotin on 3-, 6- or 12-bp stems, each incubated with Alexa Fluor 488-labeled anti-biotin IgG. Coincident events are origami-gate events (VSSC-1-H ≥ 200) with B525-H ≥ 250, and the chance expectation is  (Methods, Equation 1). Bars show the mean, error bars the SD, and points individual replicates (n = 3, except n = 2 for PLL-PEG with the 12 bp stem). Because the no-biotin samples already lie about 11-fold above the chance estimate, only the increase relative to the matched no-biotin sample is interpreted as biotin-specific binding.
