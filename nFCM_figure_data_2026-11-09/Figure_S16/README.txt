Figure S16. Streptavidin alone produces events that are positive for both side scatter and fluorescence. (A) Density plot of Alexa Fluor 488 signal (B525-H) versus violet side scatter (VSSC-1-H) for streptavidin without origami. (B) Event rates of streptavidin only and buffer for all events, events positive for both side scatter and streptavidin fluorescence (SS+ and SA+), side-scatter-positive events (SS+), and side-scatter-negative, streptavidin-positive events (SS− and SA+).
