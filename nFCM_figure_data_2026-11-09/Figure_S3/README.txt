Caption (11/09 draft):
Figure S3. (A) Negative-stain TEM (uranyl acetate) of annealed bare origami at low, medium and high magnification. (B) Aggregation on the grid: boxes with no other box within 45 nm (green), in pairs (orange) and in clusters of three or more (red), on a 600 nm field, and their fractions among 1,112 boxes in six micrographs. Random deposition at this density would place about 20% of boxes within 45 nm of another. (C) Validation of the class averages in Figure 2A: (a) boxes binned by the contrast of their weakest wall, with the contrast of the same wall on segments not used for binning; (b) random half averages and half-set Fourier ring correlation; (c) aligned member particles; (d) a reference-free 2D class (RELION 5) of the same data.
