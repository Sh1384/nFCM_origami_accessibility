Figure S4, panel B: Local slope of the cumulative side-scatter distribution of bare origami

Plotted: local slope of the cumulative count N(>=s) of raw events against VSSC-1-H, s, for bare origami without probe (three tubes pooled per batch), raw and minus the session buffer (scaled by acquisition time); central difference over 0.1 decade on a 0.05-decade grid from the trigger (200), points at 224 to 502. _raw_events.csv holds every recorded event of the eight acquisitions, unfiltered; _tubes.csv holds the slope of each tube (not plotted)

Source: fig_s4b_local_slope.py

Files: Figure_S4_B.svg, Figure_S4_B.csv, Figure_S4_B_tubes.csv, Figure_S4_B_acquisitions.csv, Figure_S4_B_raw_events.csv

Note: Suggested caption: (B) Local slope of the cumulative side-scatter distribution of bare origami, -d ln N(>=s) / d ln s, where N(>=s) is the number of recorded events with VSSC-1-H >= s. Events from three technical replicates were pooled in each batch. Slopes were calculated over windows of 0.1 decade from the trigger (200) upward, without (filled) and with (open) subtraction of buffer events scaled by acquisition time. Subtracted values are omitted where buffer accounts for at least half of the events above the window.
