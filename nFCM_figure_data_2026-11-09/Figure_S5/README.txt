Figure S5. Scatter-triggered event rate (VSSC-1-H >= 200) of serially diluted bare DNA origami as a function of nominal origami concentration, for three independent dilution chains (replicates 1 to 3) and for all three together. Solid lines are fits of R = R_buffer + kC^alpha to each chain by least squares on ln R; alpha is given as the slope with its 95% confidence interval. The dotted line is the mean rate of three buffer acquisitions. All acquisitions are shown, including five above 7,000 events/s (3.3 and 1.65 nM).
