Figure S9. Scatter–fluorescence quadrant analysis of coated origami. Percentage of events in each quadrant for (A) bare and silica-coated origami and (B) bare and PLL-PEG-coated origami in batches 1 and 2. Events were classified by side scatter (SS+ or SS−; threshold at the bare-origami 95th percentile of VSSC-1-H, 449 in batch 1 and 510 in batch 2) and by ATTO 425 fluorescence (F+ or F−; threshold at the bare-origami median V525-H, 63 in batch 1 and 73 in batch 2). Percentages are buffer-subtracted, with replicates pooled.
